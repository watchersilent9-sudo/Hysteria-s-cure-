How advancement works

Your XP increases when you answer practice questions correctly. The amount earned depends on the difficulty of the question.

Incorrect answers reduce your available points, but they do not remove your accumulated XP.

Your level changes automatically when your XP reaches the next required milestone. Hell is a stage within Genius, not a separate level.
